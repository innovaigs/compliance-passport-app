# 1. Method

1.1 Each supplier, contractor pool, and service line is scored on inherent risk (country risk, engagement model, labour intensity, data sensitivity) and control strength, producing a residual risk rating of Low, Medium, or High.

1.2 Country risk inputs are the Global Slavery Index, Transparency International CPI, and applicable sanctions lists. Scores are refreshed at least annually or on a material change.
