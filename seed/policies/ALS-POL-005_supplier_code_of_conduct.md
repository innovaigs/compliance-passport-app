# 1. Application

1.1 This Code applies to all suppliers, subcontractors, staffing partners, interpreter sourcing partners, technology vendors, and agents that provide goods or services to Altura Language Services LLC, and to their own subcontractors at every tier.

1.2 Acceptance of this Code is a condition of doing business with Altura Language Services and is incorporated by reference into supplier agreements.

# 2. Labour and Human Rights

2.1 No forced, bonded, indentured, involuntary prison, or trafficked labour. No retention of worker identity documents. No worker-paid recruitment fees at any tier — the Employer Pays Principle applies.

2.2 No child labour. Minimum age 18 for all work performed for Altura Language Services or its clients.

2.3 Wages meet or exceed applicable legal minimums, are paid on time, and are supported by clear wage statements. Unlawful deductions are prohibited.

2.4 Working hours comply with applicable law; overtime is voluntary and compensated as required by law.

2.5 Freedom of association and collective bargaining are respected. Discrimination, harassment, and abusive treatment are prohibited.

# 3. Health, Safety, and Environment

3.1 Suppliers provide a safe workplace, communicate site-specific hazards, and report serious incidents affecting Altura Language Services work within 72 hours.

3.2 Suppliers comply with applicable environmental law and support Altura Language Services reasonable requests for environmental data relating to services supplied.

# 4. Business Integrity

4.1 Zero tolerance for bribery, kickbacks, facilitation payments, or corrupt inducements, consistent with ALS-POL-003.

4.2 Compliance with applicable trade sanctions, export controls, and denied-party restrictions. Suppliers must notify Altura Language Services promptly of any listing or investigation.

4.3 Fair competition, accurate records, and prohibition on misrepresentation in bids or invoices.

# 5. Confidentiality, Data Protection, and Security

5.1 Suppliers handling Altura Language Services or client data must apply access controls on a least-privilege basis, encrypt data in transit, and restrict use strictly to the contracted purpose.

5.2 Suppliers handling protected health information in support of healthcare interpreting engagements must execute a Business Associate Agreement and comply with HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules.

5.3 Suspected data breaches affecting Altura Language Services or its clients must be reported to Altura Language Services without undue delay and in any event within 24 hours of discovery.

5.4 Interpreter confidentiality obligations survive termination of the engagement indefinitely.

# 6. Subcontracting and Flow-Down

6.1 Suppliers may not subcontract Altura Language Services work without prior written consent, and must flow down the requirements of this Code to any approved subcontractor.

# 7. Monitoring, Evidence, and Audit

7.1 Suppliers complete the Supplier Self-Assessment Questionnaire (ALS-FRM-001) at onboarding and on request thereafter, and are recorded in the Supplier Risk Register (ALS-REC-001).

7.2 Altura Language Services reserves the right to request evidence, conduct remote assessments, and, on reasonable notice, audit for compliance with this Code.

7.3 Altura Language Services's own programme is internally assessed and internally verified; no third-party certification is currently held. Suppliers are not required to hold third-party certification, but must be able to evidence the requirements of this Code.

# 8. Speak Up

8.1 Supplier personnel may report concerns about Altura Language Services or about conduct on Altura Language Services engagements directly and confidentially to ethics@alturalanguage.example under the protections of ALS-POL-004.

# 9. Consequences of Breach

9.1 Breach may result in a corrective action plan, suspension of new work, or termination of the supplier relationship. Priority is given to remedy for affected workers.

# 10. Review

10.1 Owned by the Compliance Office, approved by the Managing Member, next scheduled review 5 August 2027.
