# 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy sets out how Altura Language Services LLC treats the people who perform work for it, including the mixed workforce of United States W-2 employees and offshore independent contractors engaged as interpreters and service agents.

# 2. Freedom of Association

2.1 Altura Language Services respects the right of workers to form and join trade unions and to bargain collectively, and does not retaliate against workers who exercise those rights.

2.2 Where local law restricts freedom of association, Altura Language Services does not obstruct alternative lawful means of independent worker representation.

# 3. Non-Discrimination, Harassment, and Dignity at Work

3.1 Altura Language Services prohibits discrimination in recruitment, compensation, training, promotion, discipline, and termination.

3.2 Harassment, bullying, corporal punishment, mental or physical coercion, and verbal abuse are prohibited without exception. Reports are handled under ALS-POL-004.

3.3 Grievances may be raised with a direct manager, the Compliance Office, or anonymously via ethics@alturalanguage.example. Grievance outcomes are recorded in ALS-REC-003.

# 4. Working Hours and Rest

4.1 Working time for W-2 employees complies with the Fair Labor Standards Act and applicable state law, including overtime premium pay for non-exempt employees.

4.2 Independent contractors set their own schedules and accept or decline assignments at their discretion. Altura Language Services does not impose mandatory overtime on contractors.

4.3 Scheduling practices are designed so that no worker is required to work more than 60 hours per week including overtime, other than in exceptional and voluntary circumstances.

# 5. Wages, Benefits, and Contractor Compensation

5.1 W-2 employees are paid at or above the applicable federal, state, and local minimum wage, on a regular published pay cycle, with itemized wage statements.

5.2 Employee benefits include paid time off, employer-sponsored health coverage, an employer retirement contribution, and all statutory entitlements.

5.3 Independent contractors are paid at prevailing market rates for their language pair, certification level, and service modality, on the payment terms stated in their contract. Deductions as a disciplinary measure are prohibited.

5.4 Worker classification is reviewed against IRS and applicable state tests. Misclassification concerns may be raised through the grievance channels in section 3.3.

# 6. Health and Safety

6.1 Altura Language Services maintains a safe working environment consistent with OSHA general duty obligations for its US operations.

6.2 The majority of service delivery is remote or client-site. Where interpreters work at client sites, including healthcare settings, Altura Language Services confirms that the client's site safety and infection-control requirements have been communicated to the worker before assignment.

6.3 Work-related incidents are reported to the Compliance Office and logged in ALS-REC-002.

# 7. Community and Environmental Position

7.1 Altura Language Services operates a remote-first, low-footprint service model with no manufacturing, no hazardous materials handling, and no material process emissions.

7.2 Altura Language Services does not currently publish a quantified greenhouse gas inventory and does not hold ISO 14001 certification. Environmental claims are limited to what can be evidenced.
