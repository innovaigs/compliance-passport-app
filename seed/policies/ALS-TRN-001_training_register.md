# 1. Curriculum

| Module | Audience | Frequency | Source policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anti-trafficking and forced labour awareness | All employees and contractors | Onboarding + annual | ALS-POL-001 |
| Labour standards, anti-harassment, grievance channels | All employees and contractors | Onboarding + annual | ALS-POL-002 |
| Anti-bribery and corruption | All employees; contractors in client-facing or procurement roles | Onboarding + annual | ALS-POL-003 |
| Speak-up and non-retaliation | All employees and contractors | Onboarding + annual | ALS-POL-004 |
| Recruitment fee risk and forced labour indicators | Procurement and contractor sourcing personnel | Annual | ALS-POL-001 §4.2 |
| Confidentiality and PHI handling for interpreters | Interpreters on healthcare engagements | Onboarding + annual | ALS-POL-005 §5 |

# 2. Completion Tracking

2.1 Completion is recorded per person with module name, date completed, and version of the material. Records are retained for seven years.

2.2 Programme launched 5 August 2026. First full annual completion cycle closes 5 August 2027; population-wide completion percentages will be reportable from that date. Onboarding completion is tracked from launch.

2.3 Training material is available in English, French, and Haitian Creole.

# 3. Evidence Available to Clients

3.1 Curriculum outline, completion register extracts, and sample training materials are available to enterprise clients on request under NDA.

3.2 Training is delivered internally. No external accreditation or third-party certification is held for these modules.
