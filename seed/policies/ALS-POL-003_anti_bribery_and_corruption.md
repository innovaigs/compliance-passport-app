# 1. Purpose and Legal Framework

1.1 Altura Language Services LLC prohibits bribery and corruption in all forms. This policy is written to the standards of the US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) and the UK Bribery Act 2010, and applies globally to all Altura Language Services operations and to third parties acting on its behalf.

# 2. Prohibited Conduct

2.1 No employee, contractor, agent, or intermediary may offer, promise, give, request, agree to receive, or accept any financial or other advantage intended to induce or reward improper performance.

2.2 Facilitation payments are prohibited without exception, including small payments to expedite routine government action, notwithstanding any local custom.

2.3 Political contributions on behalf of Altura Language Services are prohibited. Charitable donations require prior written approval from the Compliance Office and are screened for corruption risk.

# 3. Gifts, Hospitality, and Entertainment

3.1 Gifts and hospitality must be modest, infrequent, transparent, not cash or cash-equivalent, and never offered or accepted during an active tender, bid, or contract negotiation.

3.2 Any gift or hospitality with an estimated value above USD 100 must be pre-approved and recorded in the Gifts and Hospitality Register (ALS-REC-002).

3.3 No gift, hospitality, or entertainment may be offered to a government official, public healthcare employee, or public procurement officer without prior written Compliance Office approval.

# 4. Third Parties and Conflicts of Interest

4.1 Agents, resellers, sourcing partners, and subcontractors are risk-screened before engagement, including sanctions and denied-party screening, and must contractually accept the Supplier Code of Conduct (ALS-POL-005).

4.2 Third-party contracts include anti-bribery representations, audit rights, and a right of termination for breach.

4.3 Actual, potential, and perceived conflicts of interest must be disclosed in writing to the Compliance Office on arising, and are recorded in the Conflicts Register (ALS-REC-002).

# 5. Books, Records, and Controls

5.1 All payments are accurately recorded in the accounting system with a description of the business purpose. Off-book accounts and unrecorded funds are prohibited.

5.2 Segregation of duties applies to payment initiation and approval. Disbursements above USD 5,000 require dual authorisation.

5.3 Records are retained for a minimum of seven years.

# 6. Training and Certification

6.1 Anti-bribery training is delivered at onboarding and annually to all employees and to contractors in client-facing or procurement-facing roles, evidenced in ALS-TRN-001.

6.2 Personnel in finance, sales, and procurement roles provide an annual written compliance certification.

# 7. Reporting

7.1 Suspected bribery or corruption must be reported via ethics@alturalanguage.example or the channels in ALS-POL-004. Non-retaliation is guaranteed under that policy.

7.2 Substantiated findings are escalated to the Managing Member and may result in termination and referral to law enforcement.

# 8. Review

8.1 Owned by the Compliance Office, approved by the Managing Member, next scheduled review 5 August 2027. The programme is internally assessed and has not been certified by an external body.
