# 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 Altura Language Services LLC ("Altura Language Services", "the Company") prohibits all forms of modern slavery, human trafficking, forced labour, bonded labour, indentured labour, and involuntary prison labour in its own operations and throughout its supply chain.

1.2 This policy applies to all directors, officers, W-2 employees, independent contractors (including offshore interpreters and agents), temporary staff, subcontractors, agencies, and suppliers acting for or on behalf of Altura Language Services.

1.3 This policy is aligned to the principles of the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, ILO Core Conventions 29 and 105, the California Transparency in Supply Chains Act, and the UK Modern Slavery Act 2015 s.54 where applicable.

# 2. Policy Statement

2.1 No worker shall be required to lodge deposits, identity documents, or passports as a condition of employment or engagement. Altura Language Services does not retain original identity documents of any worker at any time.

2.2 All work performed for Altura Language Services is voluntary. Workers are free to terminate their employment or contract with reasonable notice as defined in their agreement, without penalty.

2.3 Altura Language Services does not use recruitment fees. Where third-party recruiters or interpreter sourcing partners are used, the Employer Pays Principle applies: no worker-paid recruitment fees or related costs are permitted at any tier.

2.4 No person under the age of 18 is engaged in any capacity by Altura Language Services.

# 3. Due Diligence and Risk Assessment

3.1 Supplier and contractor onboarding requires acceptance of the Supplier Code of Conduct (ALS-POL-005) and completion of the Supplier Self-Assessment Questionnaire (ALS-FRM-001).

3.2 A documented labour rights risk assessment is maintained in the Human Rights Risk Register (ALS-REC-001), scored by country, engagement model, and service line, and reviewed at least annually.

3.3 Higher-risk engagements — defined as offshore contractor pools in countries scored elevated on the Global Slavery Index — are subject to enhanced verification including direct worker contact and contract review.

3.4 Altura Language Services has not to date engaged an independent third-party auditor or certification body for this programme. The programme is internally assessed and internally verified. This limitation is disclosed to buyers on request.

# 4. Training

4.1 All employees and contractors complete anti-trafficking and labour rights awareness training at onboarding and annually thereafter. Completion is evidenced in the Training Register (ALS-TRN-001).

4.2 Personnel with procurement or contractor-sourcing responsibility receive additional targeted training on recruitment fee risk and indicators of forced labour.

# 5. Reporting and Remediation

5.1 Suspected trafficking or forced labour must be reported immediately via the channels set out in the Whistleblower Policy (ALS-POL-004), including the confidential channel ethics@alturalanguage.example.

5.2 Confirmed findings trigger a documented remediation plan focused on remedy for the affected worker, not solely termination of the supplier relationship. Remediation actions are logged in ALS-REC-004.

# 6. Governance

6.1 The Compliance Office owns this policy. It is approved by the Managing Member and reviewed at least annually, with the next scheduled review on 5 August 2027.

6.2 Non-compliance by an employee or contractor may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or engagement.
