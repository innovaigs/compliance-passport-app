# 1. Purpose

1.1 Altura Language Services LLC encourages anyone who becomes aware of suspected misconduct connected to the Company to speak up, and protects them when they do.

# 2. Who May Report

2.1 This policy is available to employees, independent contractors, interpreters, agents, suppliers, subcontractors, clients, and members of the public.

# 3. Reportable Matters

3.1 Reportable matters include, without limitation: human trafficking or forced labour; child labour; wage or hour violations; worker misclassification; discrimination or harassment; bribery, corruption, or kickbacks; fraud, theft, or falsification of records; conflicts of interest; data privacy or confidentiality breaches; breaches of client contractual obligations; health and safety risks; and retaliation itself.

# 4. Reporting Channels

4.1 Reports may be made to a direct manager, to any member of the Compliance Office, or through the dedicated confidential channel: ethics@alturalanguage.example.

4.2 The ethics@alturalanguage.example mailbox is monitored by the Compliance Office. Reports may be submitted anonymously; anonymous reporters are asked to provide sufficient detail to permit investigation.

4.3 Reports may be submitted in English, French, or Haitian Creole. Interpretation support is provided at no cost to the reporter where another language is needed.

4.4 Nothing in this policy prevents any person from reporting directly to a government or regulatory authority, and Altura Language Services does not require notice before such a report.

# 5. Non-Retaliation

5.1 Retaliation against any person who reports a concern in good faith, or who participates in an investigation, is prohibited and is itself a disciplinary offence up to and including termination.

5.2 Protection applies even where the report is not substantiated, provided it was made in good faith.

# 6. Investigation Process

6.1 Receipt of a report is acknowledged within five business days where contact details are available.

6.2 Investigations are conducted promptly, impartially, and by persons without a conflict of interest in the matter. Where a report concerns the Managing Member, the investigation is escalated to external counsel.

6.3 Confidentiality is maintained to the fullest extent consistent with a thorough investigation and legal obligations.

6.4 Substantiated findings result in corrective and remedial action. All reports, investigation steps, outcomes, and remediation are logged in the Investigations and Remediation Record (ALS-REC-004), retained for seven years.

6.5 Aggregate reporting statistics are reviewed by the Compliance Office at least annually and are available to enterprise clients on request under NDA.

# 7. Awareness

7.1 The existence and use of these channels is communicated at onboarding, in annual training (ALS-TRN-001), and in the Supplier Code of Conduct (ALS-POL-005) issued to all suppliers and subcontractors.

# 8. Review

8.1 Owned by the Compliance Office, approved by the Managing Member, next scheduled review 5 August 2027.
